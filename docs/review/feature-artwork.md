## Creative brief

Keep all four budgies recognizable by proportions and markings. Place them together beside a garden birdhouse and shallow pond. Use a simple daisy, feather and swaying grass as observable details. Use rounded ink outlines and a muted watercolor wash on warm paper. Draw the whole family without typography.

Warm paper, sage, marigold and pale sky. Keep pose, mark placement and relative size on the illustration review sheet.

## Placement and accessible use

Keep all four faces and the head markings visible. Use contain for the quartet if the frame changes aspect ratio.

Suggested informative alt text: “Four budgies gather by a birdhouse and a garden pond, with a daisy and feather to spot.”

The author or illustrator should confirm continuity before using it as official series art. The interactive card works as text without the image.

## Export recipe

The two WebP files were generated directly from the supplied PNG, with aspect ratio preserved. The page uses the 480 and 960 px versions in srcset and declares width and height. The print stylesheet omits the full-color picture.

```sh
cwebp -resize 960 0 -q 82 art/garden-listening-trail-master.png -o art/garden-listening-trail-960.webp
cwebp -resize 480 0 -q 82 art/garden-listening-trail-master.png -o art/garden-listening-trail-480.webp
```

## Before publication

Review full size, phone width, and the real placement. Keep the original master and named web variants together. A new crop or recolor gets its own filename and a new visual review. Track the asset source, version, approval and intended placement in the site’s content record.
