## Establish the family

The four published roles are Daisy the caring, clever grown-up, Chip the curious clue-finder, Caty the careful listener and Bella the tiny new friend. The original monochrome lineup depicts a zigzag forehead, a tall crested bird with three hollow chest drops, a smooth-crowned bird with a cheek heart and a tiny star-marked fledgling. The story page also says Caty follows quiet clues along a stream. Its image alt text names the group collectively. It does not label the four positions individually. [Published character introductions](https://github.com/cephyrixzyth/crayon-kite/blob/dd6007ce69a1682e93ba968c87c7a4bbd13e3014/dist/budgie-adventures/index.html#L300) · [The Whispering River synopsis](https://github.com/cephyrixzyth/crayon-kite/blob/dd6007ce69a1682e93ba968c87c7a4bbd13e3014/dist/budgie-adventures/index.html#L254) · [Original family artwork](https://github.com/cephyrixzyth/crayon-kite/blob/dd6007ce69a1682e93ba968c87c7a4bbd13e3014/dist/assets/chirps-family.jpg)

This edition assigns the lineup, left to right, to Chip, Daisy, Caty and Bella. Adult and fledgling scale, feather shapes and the four motifs follow the existing drawing. The specific mark-to-name mapping, one-sided treatment of Caty’s heart, palette, scale targets, acting choices and dialogue are proposed production decisions. Confirm those against any author-held book models before treating them as series canon.

## Paint and staging

| Character | Primary shape | First read in monochrome | Proposed wing midtone | Acting rhythm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chip | Long, slightly forward triangle | Narrow jagged stroke down the forehead, long tail | Reed teal `#6CA9AF` | Notice → inspect → invite someone to see |
| Daisy | Tall pear, swept crest | Three hollow drops on the chest, taller than both young birds | Garden sage `#8F9B68` | Steady → lower to listen → give space |
| Caty | Round crown over a balanced oval | Single filled cheek heart, smooth brow | River periwinkle `#999DC9` | Pause → tilt toward sound → indicate direction |
| Bella | Low round body and small tail | Solid five-point forehead star | Apricot down `#DDA478` | Assess → commit → land proudly |

Use warm paper near `#F9EED7`, dark brown ink near `#322820` and warm cream faces. The hex values are mixing targets for future assets. The plates use transparent pigment with local variations. Keep the face paper-light and the identifying marks dark enough to read after desaturation. Give the eye a single clear direction. Paint wing bars with a softened edge so they remain secondary to the face. Keep tails, wing tips and toes inside the composition.

Place the cast on one ground plane when comparing scale. Use Daisy as 1.0, Chip about 0.75, Caty about 0.70 and Bella about 0.48 in a neutral stance. Measure crown to toes. The lowered, crouched and airborne poses deliberately change apparent height. The individual plates show one character at a time and each fills its own canvas. Use the cast plate for cross-character scale.

## Acting and dialogue

### Chip

Chip wants to find out how things happened. The first explanation often arrives faster than the evidence. Give Chip a second clue that changes the first guess. A good ending lets Chip explain what changed.

Shift the eyes before the body. Lower the head until the beak nearly touches the clue. A lifted foot on the return glance makes discovery feel urgent. Use a single dark zigzag rooted at the top of the forehead. Vary its foreshortening with the head angle. Keep its contour legible against the cream face.

Proposed dialogue test — “That seed rolled uphill. What moved it?”

[Chip acting master](art/chip.png) · [Web edition](art/chip.webp)

### Daisy

Daisy wants the younger birds to feel capable. Her instinct to help can make her step in a moment too soon. Show Daisy pausing long enough for a younger bird to try, then offering exactly the help the moment calls for.

Use the change in her height as the emotional beat. Keep the shoulders rounded and extend a wing low and outward to leave a friend space to move. The crest and chest carry her identity at different angles. Keep the cream chest light under the three outlined marks. A back view can rely on the crest and elongated silhouette.

Proposed dialogue test — “Let’s stop where you heard it. Show me.”

[Daisy acting master](art/daisy.png) · [Web edition](art/daisy.webp)

### Caty

Caty wants to understand the quiet part everyone else missed. She needs time to listen and a decisive moment to speak. Let Caty’s observation change the group’s route. Give another character time to hear the difference.

Close the beak for the listening beat. Turn the head before opening the wing. In the middle pose the far cheek comes into view and the heart turns out of sight. Use the same marked cheek through a sequence. Redraw a reverse angle when registering the mark. Mirroring the whole portrait would move the heart to the other cheek.

Proposed dialogue test — “Wait for the splash. The little sound comes after it.”

[Caty acting master](art/caty.png) · [Web edition](art/caty.webp)

### Bella

Bella wants to take part on her own terms. Being small changes which path looks possible. Give Bella an achievable choice with a visible consequence. Let the group follow one of her discoveries.

Compress the body before a hop, open both wings for balance, then bring the feet home. Her expression can be determined even when the obstacle is small. Keep the star solid and compact across expressions. When she looks down, rotate the star with the crown. Preserve the small bill and short tail as secondary cues.

Proposed dialogue test — “I want to try the little leaf first.”

[Bella acting master](art/bella.png) · [Web edition](art/bella.webp)

## One scene to brief a second illustrator

At the stream a seed stops moving, though the water continues. In the first view, Chip bends toward the seed and the beak leads the reader’s eye. In the next view, Caty turns toward a quieter sound under the bank. Daisy lowers her center of gravity and leaves an open space in the composition. Bella chooses a low leaf to step on and discovers the seed caught in a crease. On the last spread the others look where Bella points. This is a fictional staging exercise for this handoff. Keep the published story sample intact.

Storyboard the beats in grayscale first. On a phone-sized image, cover the captions and identify each bird. Next cover its mark and read the action from body angle, eyes and weight. At final size confirm the heart’s notch, the star’s points and all three open chest shapes survive. In a reverse angle, check which cheek is visible against the lineup. In Caty’s center acting pose the smooth far cheek is visible. In a rear view, choose silhouette, wings and tail to carry recognition.

## Asset inventory and placement

The book contains five original AI-generated watercolor-and-ink plates. The built-in image tool used the repository’s line drawing as the anatomy reference. The earlier garden scene guided the lineup’s paper and ink treatment. The new colored lineup then guided the four acting plates. Every PNG is the full generated master. Each 1200-pixel-wide WebP is an encoded derivative for screens. The PNGs are opaque. These plates work best on light paper grounds. [Generation briefs](PROMPTS.md) · [Previous garden concept](../features/art/garden-listening-trail-master.png)

1. For the Budgie Adventures hero, place the cast WebP at its natural 2:1 composition with `object-fit: contain`. Set intrinsic width and height to the actual export. Use the full cast image on narrow screens above the introductory copy. A spread or portrait crop can remove Bella or Chip.
2. For the current four emoji character introductions, keep the existing Daisy, Chip, Caty, Bella card order. Bind the art and names by stable character slug. The family image uses a different order. Show the name, role and a link to the matching full sheet. A separate illustration crop in a wide card should show the face mark and part of the body. Use an empty alt for a purely repetitive portrait next to the same text. Give the acting sheet meaningful alt text as in the book. [Existing introductions](https://github.com/cephyrixzyth/crayon-kite/blob/dd6007ce69a1682e93ba968c87c7a4bbd13e3014/dist/budgie-adventures/index.html#L300)
3. For the activity maker and printables, keep the color hero and coloring-sheet assets distinct. Trace clean, open outlines from the confirmed model. Flatten watercolor texture only for the print line-art edition. Print-test the chosen activity on Letter and A4 at normal scale.
4. For a future illustrator, commission a genuine rear and side turnaround of each approved character plus a flight silhouette sheet. The plates here show expressions and acting. They do not settle the underside of a wing, the rear markings or both sides of every character.

**Handoff check.** Open the book at phone width and on desktop. Verify that each master link opens the matching character, all three poses fit the image frame and the four-bird order matches the captions. Print the book in landscape with browser headers and footers off. Use the PNG masters for art or print layouts sized to their pixel dimensions.

## Try the integrated site

The included installer adds the proposed web artwork to a local checkout, places the colored family lineup on both landing pages and turns the four emoji cards into illustrated introductions. Each card opens into three poses and a read-aloud question. The coloring-sheet files keep their existing identities. The public-facing candidate contains only the five small WebPs and the two edited pages.

From this studio folder, run the following against a local checkout of the reviewed commit. The installer checks the target elements before writing and can be run again. Confirm the proposed cast assignments before publication.

```sh
python3 install-art.py "$CRAYON_CHECKOUT" --check
python3 install-art.py "$CRAYON_CHECKOUT"
node verify-art.cjs "$CRAYON_CHECKOUT"
python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory "$CRAYON_CHECKOUT/dist"
```
