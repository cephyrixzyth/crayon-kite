## What families can do

- Read the existing story starter in one step from home, choose its named Birdhouse Home drawing, print it and return to the excerpt. The home shelf separately identifies the optional theme song on Udio. The site loads the external player only when a reader follows its link. [Story starter](site/dist/budgie-adventures/story-starter/index.html) · [Birdhouse Home](site/dist/budgie-adventures/color/birdhouse-home/index.html)
- Choose any of ten coloring pages. Each has a shareable static page, a correctly named full-resolution download and a script-free browser-print path. The enhanced picker restores its selection after reload and browser history navigation. If a master fails to load, the picker explains how to retry and keeps its print button disabled. [Coloring shelf](site/dist/budgie-adventures/index.html#color)
- Choose a window or garden and one of three activities, then print the nature card or save its prompts. The six supplied combinations retain the listening, observation and spoken alternatives. [Nature cards](site/dist/budgie-adventures/nature-cards/index.html)
- Read the twenty supplied Welcome Tree stories and the alternate telling of story four. The reader preserves each story’s eight read-aloud passages and the discussion question. Art-production notes stay in the source packet. The illustrated character introductions use the supplied proposed lineup and acting sheets. [Welcome Tree contents](site/dist/budgie-adventures/welcome-tree/index.html) · [Character cards](site/dist/budgie-adventures/index.html#friends)

## Peer pages that informed these changes

| Source | Observed pattern | Decision in the local site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Bluey Make](https://www.bluey.tv/make/) | Collections send families directly into printable coloring sheets. | Offer one clear printable action on home and retain the individual named sheets. |
| [The Gruffalo activities](https://www.gruffalo.com/activities/) | The live collection exposed 89 distinct direct PDF targets, including picture-book activity packs. | Pair the excerpt with a named drawing and give each activity an original download. |
| [Book Dash](https://bookdash.org/) | The home page labels its reading action “Read free books.” | Name the story action explicitly and open the text in one step. |
| [Super Simple](https://supersimple.com/) | Navigation distinguishes Interactive Storybooks and Free Printables. | Keep reading, printing and optional listening separate in home navigation. |
| [The Eric Carle Museum of Picture Book Art](https://carlemuseum.org/museum-at-home/making-art) | Its Making Art index uses named, individually addressable activities, including Dragon Tales and The Bigger Picture! | Keep each sheet and story independently addressable, with a return path to the rest of the collection. |

## Additional page-level comparisons

- Storynory presents the audio, full story text, writer and narrator credits and MP3 download on the same page. The proposed narrated Chirps reader can follow this structure once a cleared recording and exact transcript are available. [Birdy’s Grudge](https://www.storynory.com/birdys-grudge/)
- Words for Life groups books for ages five to seven and supplies a listening activity for *The Missing Piece*. This provides a model for adding an age cue to the local story index and keeping a related listening action secondary. [Ten must-reads for 5 to 7-year-olds](https://wordsforlife.org.uk/activities/good-housekeepings-ten-must-reads-for-5-to-7-year-olds/)
- Twisty Noodle’s leaf collection asks families to collect real leaves to use with the printed pictures. The same page-to-outdoors connection suits the garden nature-card prompts. [Leaf activities](https://twistynoodle.com/coloring-pages/nature/leaves/)
- FirstPalette names each of six leaf sets, gives Letter and A4 printing instructions near the files, and links related crafts. Crayon Kite’s printable pages support both sizes. A related garden activity beside the Flower Garden drawing would provide a further route after printing. [Leaf Templates](https://www.firstpalette.com/printable/leaf.html)
- Dr. Seuss Enterprises separates craft, recipe, printable and guide categories and offers a smaller group of featured downloads. The distinction supports separate entries for the nature-card maker and the ten original coloring sheets. [Activities](https://www.drseuss.com/activities) · [Top Five Downloads](https://www.drseuss.com/activities/top-five-downloads)
